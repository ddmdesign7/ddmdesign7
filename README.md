## ✦ About Me

I'm **Eedris** — also known as **DDMGURU** and **ItzDDM**.

I’m interested in the space where **creative design, technology and business meet**. I enjoy shaping ideas into clear, engaging digital experiences — from visual communication and user interfaces to websites, digital tools and smarter business workflows.

My approach brings together creative thinking, practical problem-solving and a focus on the people using what we build.
